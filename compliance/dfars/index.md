## What Is Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS)?

The Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) is a set of contract clauses used by the Department of Defense (DoD) to supplement the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR). These regulations govern various aspects of defense contracts, including hiring practices, product quality, and supply chain policies.

One key requirement, DFARS 252.204-7012, mandates compliance with [NIST 800-171](/compliance/nist-800-171/), a catalog of 110 security controls derived from [NIST 800-53](/compliance/nist-800-53/). This [ensures companies adhere](/government-it-solutions-dfars-252-204-7012/) to stringent cybersecurity standards, covering policies, procedures, and technologies to protect sensitive information. Reviewing non-DFARS requirements can provide additional insights into these regulations.
