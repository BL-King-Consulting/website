## What Is ISO Compliance?

Hackers have stolen billions of private files from companies in the past few years, from credit card numbers to medical records. No business is immune—if your information is easily accessible, hackers don’t care if you’re a small business or a Fortune 500 company. While there’s no foolproof way to keep your data 100% protected from information security risks, you can significantly reduce your chances of data loss by implementing ISO 27001 standards.

The International Organization for Standardization, or ISO, created the 27001 standards in 2005. These standards act as a framework for businesses to protect their information assets. Since then, ISO 27001 has become internationally recognized as the gold standard for cyber risk management. As a result, many companies require contractors to prove ISO 27001 compliance before agreeing to work together.

Achieving ISO compliance can help you gain trust with clients, especially if you collect and store their private information. Receiving ISO 27001 certification demonstrates that you underwent a complete security overhaul and passed a rigorous assessment to prove you have extensive security controls in place. Even if you don’t need to be ISO 27001 compliant, doing so can give you the upper hand over other businesses.

### Understanding ISO 27001 Certification Requirements

The ISO 27001 standard is unique because you don’t have to comply with every information security control to achieve certification. Instead, you have to determine what information needs protection and come up with a plan for your information security management system (ISMS). Then, you must evaluate your threats and vulnerabilities using an ISO 27001 risk assessment. Finally, ISO 27001 certification requires you to develop a plan to treat those risks.

Because ISO 27001 compliance looks different for every organization, BL King Consulting develops a tailored plan to meet your security needs.
