## Have Peace of Mind With BL King’s Network Monitoring Services

If you’re like most of our clients, you might be wondering, what is network monitoring? When you log in on your work computer each morning, you probably don’t think about everything that’s going on in the background to make your network functional. What you see is made possible by a complex web of elements that work in tandem to make your technology functional. If one of those elements stops working, it can throw off your entire network.

Utilizing BL King’s network monitoring services catches issues in real-time and fixes them before they turn into a larger problem. Add in the overall day-to-day management of your network, and you can keep your business systems up and running in even the most demanding IT environments.

BL King Consulting uses security information and event management (SIEM) software to monitor incoming and outgoing traffic from your applications, intrusion prevention systems, firewalls, and software. [Depending on your needs](/cybersecurity/), we can remotely monitor your network constantly or check your event logs on a regular basis to identify security threats. With our threat detection and response services, you can prevent data breaches, minimize other security events, and keep your network safe with little to no effort on your team’s part.
