## Align Cybersecurity With Your Business Objectives With Expert vCISO Support

*Also known as Fractional CISO — the terms describe the same part-time, executive-level security leadership model.*

Cybersecurity shouldn’t be siloed—it should be driving conversations in the boardroom. BL King’s Virtual Chief Information Security Officer (vCISO) service delivers high-level security leadership without the overhead of a full-time hire. Our vCISOs work hand-in-hand with executive teams to align security initiatives with business strategy, improve resilience, and support long-term growth.

Whether you’re navigating regulatory frameworks like CMMC or NIST, or need to assess and harden your risk posture, we embed with your team to guide decisions from a position of expertise, not guesswork. It’s cybersecurity as a strategic asset, not just a line item.
