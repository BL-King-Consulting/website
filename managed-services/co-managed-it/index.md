### The Co-Managed IT Model Defined

A co-managed IT model is the best of both worlds. Co-managed IT blends an internal team’s strategic oversight, experience, and control with the flexibility, expertise, and resources of an outsourced provider. In a co-managed model, you collaborate to decide how you’d like the relationship to look. Good communication is the key to a successful co-managed system. You need an accessible, responsive partner who effectively communicates with your internal IT team.

A co-managed IT model offers the greatest flexibility in IT. Co-managed models let you choose what aspects of your business you’d like bolstered by the outsourced provider. For premier IT services customized to your needs and communication, choose BL King.
