## What You Can Expect With BL King

Our IT help desk services are scoped to fit your environment, your team, and the way your business actually operates. Here’s what that looks like in practice.

### End User Support

At BL King, our end-user support covers the full range of day-to-day technical issues your employees encounter, from software errors and connectivity problems to hardware failures and account access issues. Whether your team is in the office or working remotely, they get the same level of responsive, reliable support wherever they are.

### IT Ticketing System and Ticket Management

Every issue that comes through our service desk is logged, tracked, and managed through a structured IT ticketing system, so nothing falls through the cracks and every request has an owner. We track resolution times, identify recurring problems, and use that data to continuously improve your support experience over time.

### Remote and Onsite IT Support

Most issues are resolved remotely, and we’re equipped to do that quickly and securely. When a problem requires hands-on attention, BL King technicians are available for onsite support when needed, giving you the efficiency of remote resolution backed by the reliability of local coverage.

### Escalation and Tier-Based Support

Not every issue belongs at the same support tier, and at BL King, we don’t treat them that way. Our escalation process routes problems to the right level of expertise without delay, moving routine requests through quickly and escalating complex issues to senior technicians or our cybersecurity team when needed. You don’t have to manage that process. We do.
