## What is a Security Operations Center?

As cyberthreats continue to grow in complexity and sophistication, so must your cybersecurity solution. It’s no longer enough to rely solely on antivirus software or a firewall. Your organization needs multifaceted cybersecurity that’s capable of protecting your business from all sides. Get the advanced cybersecurity your company deserves with BL King’s security operations center (SOC) services.

A security operations center refers to a specialized team of cybersecurity analysts that focuses on threat detection and incident response. By conducting these security operations, our team proactively eliminates threats before they mature into something that could cause damage to your network. As a result, BL King greatly strengthens the security posture of your business.

### What’s the Purpose of a Security Operations Center?

Our function as an SOC provider is to monitor, detect, investigate, and respond to cyberthreats. We are dedicated to [monitoring](/managed-services/network-monitoring/) and protecting your intellectual property, personnel data, business systems, and any other important assets in danger of a cyberattack. Our service acts as a central point of collaboration in a coordinated effort to protect your network and devices. As your partner, we become an integral part of your cybersecurity framework.

### How Does a Cybersecurity Operations Center Work?

Security operations center as a service is a managed services offering where you partner with our team of highly trained cybersecurity analysts. Our experts use security operations center best practices to thoroughly monitor, detect, and investigate threats across your entire enterprise. If we discover suspicious activity, we act quickly to remediate issues so there’s minimal disruption. If you have an internal IT department, we work closely with them to implement the best security solutions for your business.

## Why Do You Need A Security Operations Center?

There’s a strong push toward digital transformation in the business world as technology enables better efficiency and productivity. However, as you take advantage of the cloud, devices, and collaboration tools, the higher the risk is of becoming a cyberattack victim. A data breach not only compromises your network, but it can also cause significant collateral damage in the form of fines, ruined reputation, and data loss.

Our SOC team is determined to do everything in our power to prevent data breaches from occurring. To accomplish this goal, we implement security procedures and deploy various security tools. This allows us to take the appropriate steps to mitigate trouble as soon as any abnormality is detected. Having an SOC is essential to maintaining a strong security posture.
