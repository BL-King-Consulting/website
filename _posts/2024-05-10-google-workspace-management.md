## Understanding Google Workspace

The definition of “work” is changing. Decades ago, work was a leather briefcase, a cubicle in an office with fluorescent lighting, and thousands of paper documents piled into filing cabinets. Later on, we digitized our paper documents, got rid of bulky cabinets, and traded briefcases for laptop bags. Now, work takes many forms—video conference calls from our couches, client conversations in our cars, and in-office conversations over messaging platforms. As the definition of work changes, businesses have to adapt to new challenges. The technology you used a few years ago may not work in a remote environment. Your security controls may work for your on-premises server but not your new cloud infrastructure. If your technology is outdated, you need a digital transformation.

Luckily, Google Workspace makes it easy to adapt to an increasingly digital world. Google Workspace is a cloud-based toolkit that includes all the applications you and your employees need to work from any location. From scheduling video meetings with coworkers to collaborating on a digital document, you can complete your most essential tasks using the Google cloud platform. [Migrating your data and processes](/the-complete-guide-to-google-workspace-support/) to [Google Workspace](/benefits-of-purchasing-microsoft-google-workspace-through-a-reseller/) can be overwhelming, but BL King Consulting can help. We make the transition seamless, so you don’t experience an interruption in your work

### Workspace Applications

When you purchase Google Workspace for your office, you gain access to a suite of tools that work together. For example, your collaborators receive an email notification if you comment on a document in the Google Docs, Slides, or Sheets app. You can join a video meeting on the Meet app from Google Calendar. In addition to communication and document creation apps, Google Workspace comes with apps for website creation, user account management, document archival, and software integration. If you need a tool to improve collaboration and productivity, Workspace probably has it.

### Google Cloud Security Features

At BL King Consulting, we only recommend the most secure platforms to our customers. Google Workspace, with its state-of-the-art security features, exceeds our standards. The platform stores your data in a safe cloud environment across a network of data centers. Plus, Google runs its own operating system, which gives its team complete control over Workspace security features. Because of these cutting-edge security features, Workspace is compliant with various standards, including [ISO 27001](/compliance/iso/), and is certified FedRAMP Moderate.
