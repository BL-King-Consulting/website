## Why DFARS 252.204-7020 Has a New Number

DFARS 252.204-7020 is the clause that covers government assessments of your NIST SP 800-171 implementation. For new DoD solicitations and contracts that use Class Deviation 2026-O0025, it now appears as DFARS 252.240-7997, "NIST SP 800-171 DoD Assessment Requirements." It is the only commonly cited DFARS cybersecurity clause that got a new number.

The change comes from the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul. On December 18, 2025, the Department began issuing class deviations that rewrite the DFARS one part at a time, all effective February 1, 2026 ([Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/DFARS-Overhaul-Class-Deviations)). The deviation for information security, 2026-O0025, tells contracting officers to use a new DFARS Part 240 and the revised FAR Part 40 in place of the text printed in the Code of Federal Regulations ([DoD deviation memo](https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/classdev/DFARS_RFO/Part-240/2026-O0025_TAB_A_Deviation_Memo_DFARS_240.pdf)).

The cybersecurity sections moved. Former DFARS Subpart 204.73 now sits at DFARS 240.370, and the CMMC section, formerly 204.75, now sits at 240.371 ([Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/DFARS-Overhaul-Class-Deviations)). Most of the clauses those sections prescribe did not follow them to new numbers.

## What Changed and What Did Not

| Clause | Old number | Under Deviation 2026-O0025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Safeguarding Covered Defense Information and Cyber Incident Reporting | <span style="white-space: nowrap">252.204-7012</span> | Same number. Now names NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2 explicitly. |
| NIST SP 800-171 DoD Assessment Requirements | <span style="white-space: nowrap">252.204-7020</span> | Renumbered to <span style="white-space: nowrap">252.240-7997</span>. Basic assessment references removed. |
| Notice of NIST SP 800-171 DoD Assessment Requirements (solicitation provision) | <span style="white-space: nowrap">252.204-7019</span> | Dropped from the deviation text. |
| CMMC Level Requirements | <span style="white-space: nowrap">252.204-7021</span> | Same number. |
| Notice of CMMC Level Requirements | <span style="white-space: nowrap">252.204-7025</span> | Same number. |
| Basic Safeguarding of Covered Contractor Information Systems | <span style="white-space: nowrap">FAR 52.204-21</span> | Appears as <span style="white-space: nowrap">FAR 52.240-93</span>. Same 15 requirements. |

All numbers are DFARS clauses unless marked FAR.

Sources for the table: [Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/DFARS-Overhaul-Class-Deviations) on the 7012, 7020 and 7021 changes; [Miles & Stockbridge](https://www.mslaw.com/cybersecurity-emerging-technologies-government-contracts-compliance/client-alerts/dod-reorganizes-cybersecurity-clauses-in-follow-up-to-far-overhaul) on 7019, 7020, 7025 and FAR 52.240-93.

### 252.204-7012 Kept Its Number

Wiley notes that the Part 240 clauses "have mostly retained their numbering from the old DFARS Part 204," and gives 252.204-7012 and 252.204-7021 as examples ([Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/DFARS-Overhaul-Class-Deviations)). The one text change Wiley reports in 7012 is that it now says "Revision 2" when it points to the NIST SP 800-171 controls.

Your duties under 7012 are the same. You still have to provide adequate security on covered systems and report cyber incidents ([Miles & Stockbridge](https://www.mslaw.com/cybersecurity-emerging-technologies-government-contracts-compliance/client-alerts/dod-reorganizes-cybersecurity-clauses-in-follow-up-to-far-overhaul)). The CMMC Phase 2 pause did not change that either. Revision 3 of the deviation, dated September 3, 2026, still requires baseline compliance with NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2 under 7012 ([Dentons](https://www.dentons.com/en/insights/alerts/2026/october/22/cmmc-phase-ii-follow-up)).

### 252.240-7997 Drops the Basic Assessment

The renumbered clause also changed in content. Miles & Stockbridge reports that "the updated version eliminates all references to Basic Assessments" ([Miles & Stockbridge](https://www.mslaw.com/cybersecurity-emerging-technologies-government-contracts-compliance/client-alerts/dod-reorganizes-cybersecurity-clauses-in-follow-up-to-far-overhaul)). What remains covers Medium and High assessments done by government staff using NIST SP 800-171A, with summary scores posted in SPRS and 14 business days for you to rebut findings before they post ([Serabrynn](https://serabrynn.com/resources/cmmc-phase-2-suspension-class-deviation-revision-3/)).

Self-assessment did not go away. Miles & Stockbridge says the requirements that used to sit in 7019 "are solely addressed under the CMMC framework" now, and that 7021 still requires a current CMMC status in SPRS and an annual affirmation ([Miles & Stockbridge](https://www.mslaw.com/cybersecurity-emerging-technologies-government-contracts-compliance/client-alerts/dod-reorganizes-cybersecurity-clauses-in-follow-up-to-far-overhaul)).

## Why You Will See Both Numbers

A class deviation is a temporary instruction to contracting officers. It does not rewrite the printed regulation. The codified DFARS still carries the old clauses until final rulemaking, so both sets of numbers stay in circulation ([Abel Solutions](https://www.abelsolutions.com/dfars-compliance/)). The deviation stays in effect until it is rescinded or folded into the FAR, DFARS and PGI ([DoD deviation memo](https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/classdev/DFARS_RFO/Part-240/2026-O0025_TAB_A_Deviation_Memo_DFARS_240.pdf)).

In practice:

- A contract or subcontract signed before February 2026 will usually still list 252.204-7020.
- A solicitation issued since then may list 252.240-7997, and the clause title carries the tag "(DEVIATION 2026-O0025)" ([Abel Solutions](https://www.abelsolutions.com/dfars-compliance/)).
- Miles & Stockbridge advises contractors to "be prepared to see a mix of new and old provisions and clauses" ([Miles & Stockbridge](https://www.mslaw.com/cybersecurity-emerging-technologies-government-contracts-compliance/client-alerts/dod-reorganizes-cybersecurity-clauses-in-follow-up-to-far-overhaul)).

Neither number is wrong. The number that applies to you is the one printed in your contract.

## What to Check This Week

1. **Read the clause list in each prime contract and subcontract.** Note whether it says 252.204-7020 or 252.240-7997, and whether you see 52.204-21 or 52.240-93.
2. **Update your System Security Plan references.** If your SSP cites only 252.204-7020, add the new number next to it, for example "DFARS 252.240-7997 (formerly 252.204-7020)," so a reviewer on a newer contract can match it.
3. **Check your flow-down language.** If you are a prime or a higher-tier sub, make sure the clauses you pass down match the version in your own contract.
4. **Confirm your SPRS entries.** 7021 still expects a current CMMC status and an annual affirmation where your contract specifies a CMMC level.
5. **Do not wait on 7012.** NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2 is still the baseline, pause or no pause.
