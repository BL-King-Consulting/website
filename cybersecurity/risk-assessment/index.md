## Who Needs a Cybersecurity Risk Assessment?

Hackers don’t care about the size of your business or the type of work you do. If your network could be vulnerable and criminals have something to gain from stealing your information, you would benefit from a cybersecurity risk assessment. Scheduling a quick assessment today could save you from spending six figures cleaning up a cyberattack in the future.

![IT Technicians Consulting Data on Tablet](/assets/images/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/IT-Technicians-Consulting-Data-on-Tablet.jpg)

If your business contracts with the Department of Defense, you don’t have a choice in protecting your data. Anyone handling federal contract information or Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) must follow the [Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC)](/compliance/cmmc/). We also provide:

- **Vulnerability Assessments**: BL King Consulting conducts thorough vulnerability assessments, initiating a conversation to grasp your network’s current security framework, ensuring a comprehensive understanding before proceeding to an extensive gap analysis.

- **IT Security Gap Analysis**: Following a vulnerability analysis, BL King Consulting devises a tailored action plan, conducting either a comprehensive cybersecurity assessment or an [IT security gap analysis](/compliance/gap-analysis/) based on your organization’s needs and compliance standards.
