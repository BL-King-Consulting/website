### Why You Need Cybersecurity Training for Your Employees

Every business that wants to minimize security threats and protect its bank account can benefit from IT security training. However, some companies need IT security training to maintain compliance with regulations. If you are a Department of Defense contractor required to comply with the [Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC)](/compliance/cmmc/), you need to conduct security awareness training. The [NIST](/compliance/nist-800-171/) and [ISO 27001](/compliance/iso/) standards also require companies to train their employees about proper cybersecurity practices.
