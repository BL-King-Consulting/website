## Choose BL King as Your Provider for Cybersecurity Solutions for Small Businesses

If [cybersecurity](/small-business-cybersecurity-how-hackers-get-data/) doesn’t have the top spot on your list of IT concerns, it should. In years past, you might have been able to get away with a lax information security approach, but things are different today. Not only are cybercriminals getting craftier with their attacks, but companies are relying more and more on technology to perform their most essential functions.

This combination of risks can be lethal to your business. The average cost of a cyber incident is $200,000, which is enough to close many small and medium-sized companies for good. If you’re not focusing on cybersecurity, you’re taking a gamble on your company’s future.

At BL King Consulting, IT security consulting isn’t just one of our offerings—it provides the framework for everything we do. Whether we step in as your [desktop support team](/managed-services/it-help-desk-services/) or migrate your data to the cloud, our top priority is keeping your confidential data out of the wrong hands.

Cybersecurity is [an ongoing effort](/cto-guide-to-cybersecurity-roadmap/). Installing a firewall or implementing multi-factor authentication may make your technology safer today, but those are just steps in your security journey. That’s why BL King Consulting offers services throughout the entire security lifecycle, beginning with a risk assessment and continuing with ongoing managed cybersecurity services. We make your network safer now while helping you prepare for security risks in the future.

## Cybersecurity Management Life Cycle

At BL King Consulting, we follow the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) framework to ensure our clients achieve the highest security standards. We use these five functions to provide immediate security safeguards and prepare your business for worst-case scenarios:

- **Identify**: Using our [cybersecurity risk assessment](/cybersecurity/risk-assessment/), we identify your most pressing security risks and develop an action plan for remediation.

- **Protect**: We protect your business from immediate harm by adding intrusion-prevention solutions to your network.

- **Detect**: Our team members manage and [monitor your network](/managed-services/network-monitoring/) daily to catch unusual activity before it affects it.

- **Respond**: If a cyberattack occurs, we help you follow your disaster recovery plan and minimize the damage to your systems.

- **Recover**: Because we focus on creating proactive plans for potential disaster scenarios, we back up and safeguard your data so it’s easy to restore.
