## Cybersecurity and IT Support Built Around Your Organization

At BL King Consulting, we don't believe in forcing every business into the same fixed package. Whether you're just getting started with [NIST 800-171](/compliance/nist-800-171/) readiness or preparing for a full-scale [CMMC](/compliance/cmmc/) audit, our engagement scales with your infrastructure, your compliance goals, and how hands-on you want our team to be — from guiding your internal staff with essential tools and best practices, to fully managing your cybersecurity and compliance as your outsourced team.

## Core Cybersecurity Protection

- **eXtended Detection and Response (XDR) / Security Operations Center (SOC) Monitoring**: Our XDR technology monitors your systems for signs of intrusions and actively prevents them. By correlating attacks across devices and log files, we enhance threat-hunting capabilities and reduce response times.

- **Threat Intelligence Enrichment**: We fuse incoming activity logs with known malicious data to identify high-priority alerts and threats swiftly.

- **Network Device Management**: Our team provides configuration, maintenance, and patch management for all network devices, ensuring readiness against threats.

- **Tailored Cybersecurity Training, Advanced Phishing Testing, and Employee Breach Monitoring**: We offer personalized training, phishing testing, and dark web monitoring to enhance employee awareness and prevent data breaches.

- **OS and 3rd Party Patch Management**: We ensure prompt application of patches to mitigate vulnerabilities and reduce the risk of successful attacks.

- **Identity and Access Management**: Our solutions manage identities, prevent credential compromise, and minimize access authorization creep.

## Enhanced Monitoring and Incident Response

- **Unlimited Incident Response, Planning, and Testing**: Our robust Incident Response Plan provides rapid notification and detailed reporting, ensuring effective response to cyber incidents.

- **24/7 Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR)**: Continuous monitoring and live response across your endpoints.

- **DNS Filtering**: We employ DNS filtering to block requests to malicious domains, preventing unwanted activity at its source.

- **Computer Security Hardening**: We harden servers and workstations to prevent malware injection and unauthorized system modifications.

- **Firewall Management and Identity Access Control**: Ongoing management to keep your perimeter and access controls current as threats evolve.

- **Vulnerability Assessment (Blue Team Penetration Test)**: Our vulnerability assessments prioritize identified vulnerabilities based on severity and exploitability.

## Comprehensive Compliance and Risk Management

- **Cloud Collaboration and Cloud VM Monitoring**: We monitor cloud collaboration platforms and virtual machines to mitigate data risks and prevent system compromise.

- **Risk Assessments**: Our risk assessments identify vulnerabilities and threats across your operations, including cloud-based services.

- **Configuration Management and Auditing**: We audit software additions and ensure compliance with approved software baselines to mitigate the risk of rogue software installations.

- **Security Assessments and Continuous Monitoring**: We conduct gap analysis and audit security controls on a rolling basis to ensure effectiveness and compliance with regulatory requirements.

- **Policy Development, Management, and Refinement**: We develop, manage, and refine cybersecurity policies to communicate and enforce security protections effectively.

- **Fully Managed Compliance Support**: For organizations who want us to take full control, we serve as your outsourced security and compliance team — handling audits, documentation, response plans, training, and reporting across your entire environment.
