## Our Mindset

BL King Consulting is a service-disabled veteran-owned small business, and our veteran mindset is what makes our company stand out. After serving as a security officer in the U.S. Navy for five years, our owner and founder spent nearly 10 years working for Department of Defense (DoD) contractors as a security specialist. He used his extensive experience to open BL King Consulting in 2014, which started as a compliance consulting firm serving DoD contractors.

Since then, our veteran mentality has enabled us to grow our client base and expand into managed cybersecurity services and IT project work. We apply the same values our founder learned in the Navy—drive, teamwork, integrity, and excellence—to our client relationships. Whereas other companies try to find quick solutions and upselling opportunities, we earn our clients’ trust by providing customized solutions within their budgets.

If you want to work with a company known for honesty and commitment, BL King Consulting is the right place for you.

## Our Comprehensive Benefits

So many companies claim to have a “competitive benefits package” that this phrase has completely lost its meaning. At BL King Consulting, our benefits aren’t just competitive—they’re some of the best you can find. We want our team members to feel valued as people, not just employees, which is why we offer the following:

- 100% paid medical coverage

- Company-provided long-term disability coverage

- Individual retirement account (IRA) plan with 3% company match

- 10 paid holidays

- Three weeks of PTO to start

- Flexible work schedule

- Fully remote work options (unless you work onsite at a client’s office)

Your insurance, retirement plan, and flexible work options aren’t the only perks you get as a BL King Consulting employee. You also get to learn from experienced compliance professionals, work with a close-knit team of other talented individuals, and serve a diverse group of clients nationwide.
