## Our Story

BL King Consulting officially opened in 2014, but our roots trace back to when our owner joined the Navy as a security officer over 20 years ago. During his service, he developed many of the skills he uses at BL King Consulting today, including an advanced understanding of [cybersecurity](/cybersecurity/) and regulatory [compliance](/compliance/). Additionally, as a veteran, our owner brings a unique energy to the IT space and emphasizes teamwork, discipline, integrity, and work ethic.

Since 2014, we have become a certified service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB) and provided cybersecurity problem-solving for commercial clients and U.S. government contractors. Additionally, our team has served as a cybersecurity consultant for several high-profile government clients, including the Department of Defense (DoD), Department of Commerce, and Department of Veteran Affairs.

BL King is known for our straightforward approach to IT. Our team members are some of the most knowledgeable security experts in the business, which means you never have to wait for us to find the right solution. We get things done quickly, affordably, and most importantly—we do things the right way the first time.

## CMMC In A Box

Bobby King, our CEO, designed CMMC In a Box, a compliance solution that can help even small defense contractors get audit-ready in as little as 60 days affordably. Practically, what it includes is:

- A near-compliant physical or virtual computer shipped air-priority to your business.
- Customization of the remaining policies to your business and preferences.
- A branded email that you can use to send and receive CUI.
- Ongoing CMMC support both during and after your audit to make sure you pass on the first try and stay compliant once you’re certified.

It’s an all-in-one package for any business of any size in any industry to get audit-ready in as little as 60 days affordably, pass the audit on the first try, and then forget about CMMC so you can focus on whatever your real passion is.
